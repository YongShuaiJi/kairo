Thank you for contributing to Kairo.

## Contributor representations

By submitting a contribution, you represent that:

- you created the contribution or otherwise have sufficient rights to submit and license it;
- the contribution does not knowingly include third-party code, documentation, assets, or confidential
  information without compatible permission and attribution;
- you have complied with any employer, client, school, or other obligation that may affect ownership;
- you have reviewed and take responsibility for the submitted result, including any part created with the
  assistance of generative AI or code-generation tools;
- third-party material and generated files are identified with their applicable source and license.

Do not remove existing copyright, license, or third-party attribution notices.

## Development expectations

- Keep changes focused and preserve module boundaries.
- Add tests proportional to the behavior and risk being changed.
- Update API, user, developer, and operations documentation when relevant.
- Do not include secrets, proprietary customer data, or code copied from an unknown source.
- Explain material use of generated code when provenance or licensing may not be obvious to reviewers.
